How to Measure

Height

Stand straight against a wall without shoes.

Shoulder

Measure from the tip of one shoulder bone to the other.

Chest / Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your chest.

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline (narrowest part).

Hip

Measure around the fullest part of your hips.
